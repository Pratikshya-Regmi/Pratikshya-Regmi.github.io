# Reverse chronology

All dated collection lists now use `astro-site/src/utils/chronology.ts`: ongoing entries first, then the latest documented year and month, newest first. Recurring awards use their most recent year. Equal dates preserve editorial order; year-only records follow month-specific records of the same year, without asserting an unknown month. Undated entries follow dated entries.

Education, experience, presentations, publications, workshops delivered, honors, certifications, project lists, homepage highlights and workshop media links use this rule. Service bullets and the public CV were reordered with ongoing appointments first. Hackathons and volunteering were checked and already list newest activities first within their respective groups. Community content retains its topic groupings.

Publication years come from existing citations (the UASG 2019 chapter was published in 2020). Certification months come from the existing CV. Project labels explicitly identify presentation/thesis years when project dates are unavailable; no research start/end dates were invented. ORNL retains May–August 2026.

Existing unresolved source conflicts remain unresolved: ITRE date range, CBI scholarship year, and the CV PhD institution line's ambiguous 2025 versus the site's Fall 2023–present attendance. No unsupported correction was made. Same-year presentations without months cannot be ordered more precisely until dates are confirmed.

CV verification compares all words and URI links before/after to prevent content loss; both public CV copies are updated. No consolidation, commit, push or deployment is part of this change.
