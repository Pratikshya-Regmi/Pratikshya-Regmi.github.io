## Research question
How can forest resistance, delayed canopy loss, and early recovery following Hurricane Helene be distinguished?

## Research context
This project contributes to my broader research on how extreme events influence forest structure and recovery, connecting observations of canopy change with questions about ecosystem resilience.

## My role
Intern at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, May–August 2026.

## Conceptual framework
The figure separates the immediate change from the pre-storm canopy baseline from two possible subsequent trajectories: recovery or further canopy loss, labeled “delayed mortality” in the diagram. Its vertical axis represents relative canopy structure or biomass.

The framework compares repeat LiDAR observations across forest areas with different levels of storm damage and subsequent canopy loss.
